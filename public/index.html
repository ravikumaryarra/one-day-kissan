Location 01 • Main Hub

Bhongir

Yadadri Bhuvanagiri • Telangana
Hotel Vivera, Hyderabad–Warangal Highway, Bhongir Bypass, Bhongir, Telangana – 508116.
Travel: ~55 km from Hyderabad (60–75 min via NH 163 Warangal Highway). Direct highway bypass access.
  • Farm Terroir: Expansive wetland paddy fields, traditional canal irrigation & historic Deccan boulder scenery.
  • Core Highlights: Working rice mill lifecycle, desi cattle ploughing, potter's wheel, and open mud sports grounds.
  • Verified Facilities: Shaded dining pavilions (seats 300+), clean washrooms, potable RO water & bus parking.

LOCATION 02 Branch Farm
Location 02 • Agro-Forest Hub

Moinabad

Ranga Reddy • Telangana
Kapil Agro Farms, Sriramnagar, Moinabad, Telangana – 501504.
Travel: ~25 km from Gachibowli / Financial District (40–50 min via Chevella Road). Convenient weekend retreat.
  • Farm Terroir: Verdant agro-orchards, mango groves, coconut canopies & bio-rich compost soil patches.
  • Core Highlights: Exotic and native bird aviary, country tractor trails, vegetable picking, and farm-to-fork trails.
  • Verified Facilities: Shaded outdoor pavilion, clean washrooms, potable water, and dedicated coach parking.

We welcome visitors at two beautiful Telangana locations: our Main Farm near Hotel Vivera (Hyderabad - Warangal Highway), Bhongir Bypass, Bhongir - 508 116, and our Branch Farm at Kapil Agro Farms, Sriramnagar, Moinabad, Telangana - 501 504. Both farms are an easy, pleasant drive from Hyderabad, making either an ideal countryside day-trip destination.

One Day Kisan is thoughtfully crafted for school field trips, college student groups, families with children, friend circles, corporate team retreats, and weekend picnics. We welcome visitors of all age groups and tailor field steward guidance accordingly.

We offer 23+ immersive farm activities including Tractor Joy Ride, Bullock Cart Experience, Ploughing, Paddy Transplantation, Clay Pottery Wheel, Dung Cake Making, Earthworm / Vermicompost Pit, Scarecrow Making, Persian Wheel, Archimedes Screw, and traditional water lifting heritage.

Visitors and school groups are warmly welcome to bring their own picnic food and refreshments. For larger groups, schools, and corporate team outings, authentic regional farm meals and catering options can be arranged with prior notice—please reach out to our team when booking.

You can book directly using the online reservation wizard on this website, call or WhatsApp our team at +91 98497 98674, or email info@onedaykisan.com. We recommend booking at least a week in advance for school field trips and large institutional groups.

Absolutely! Our activities are designed to be safe, tactile, and highly engaging for children from kindergarten age upwards. Dedicated farm stewards guide every session to ensure young explorers have an enriching, joyful, and completely safe experience.

We do not provide direct transportation, but both our Bhongir farm (on the Hyderabad–Warangal Highway) and Moinabad farm (Chevella Road) are directly accessible via well-paved highways with ample coach parking for school buses and private vehicles. Contact us for precise gate directions.

We recommend comfortable cotton casual clothing and walking shoes or sandals that can be easily slipped off for soil and mud paddy activities. An extra pair of clothes is advised for children. Sun hats or caps and reusable water bottles are strongly recommended for sunny outdoor hours.
