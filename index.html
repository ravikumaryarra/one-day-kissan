Location 01 • Main Hub

Bhongir

Yadadri Bhuvanagiri • Telangana
Hotel Vivera, Hyderabad–Warangal Highway, Bhongir Bypass, Bhongir, Telangana – 508116.
Travel: ~55 km from Hyderabad (60–75 min via NH 163 Warangal Highway). Direct highway bypass access.
  • Farm Terroir: Expansive wetland paddy fields, traditional canal irrigation & historic Deccan boulder scenery.
  • Core Highlights: Working rice mill lifecycle, desi cattle ploughing, potter's wheel, and open mud sports grounds.
  • Verified Facilities: Shaded dining pavilions (seats 300+), clean washrooms, potable RO water & bus parking.

LOCATION 02 Branch Farm
Location 02 • Agro-Forest Hub

Moinabad

Ranga Reddy • Telangana
Kapil Agro Farms, Sriramnagar, Moinabad, Telangana – 501504.
Travel: ~25 km from Gachibowli / Financial District (40–50 min via Chevella Road). Convenient weekend retreat.
  • Farm Terroir: Verdant agro-orchards, mango groves, coconut canopies & bio-rich compost soil patches.
  • Core Highlights: Exotic and native bird aviary, country tractor trails, vegetable picking, and farm-to-fork trails.
  • Verified Facilities: Shaded outdoor pavilion, clean washrooms, potable water, and dedicated coach parking.

Essential Information for Your Farm Day

Helpful guidelines to ensure a comfortable, joyful countryside immersion.

23+ Farm Activities

Paddy planting, bullock cart rides, tractor trails, pottery, desi cattle petting, and traditional village games.

Suitable for Everyone

Thoughtfully tailored itineraries for school batches, multi-generational families, friends, and corporate retreats.

Food & Picnics

Visitors are welcome to bring home-cooked food for outdoor picnics. Authentic village catering can be arranged upon request.

Attire & Footwear

Wear comfortable cotton clothing and closed-toe footwear suitable for walking through mud, earth, and farming paths.
